# v2.4.2

The two-tap contract on the Touch roll-entry grid is now visible in
color (issue #10). Previously both interaction states rendered
identically — a tapped cell went green, and confirming it (second tap on
the same cell, or BTN2) gave no feedback at all; the screen was simply
the next roll.

Now the first tap (selection) lights the chosen cell in bold white — a
double white border around the white digit — and the confirm flashes the
cell green for ~250 ms before the screen advances. Both commit paths
flash identically (one shared `confirmCurrentRoll()`). Green now means
exactly one thing on this screen: "this value just entered rolls[]".

Deliberately scoped to the roll grid: the menu and quiz cells and the
non-touch big digit keep their established white-unselected/green-
selected language, and non-Touch boards are unchanged. No change to the
seed algorithm.
