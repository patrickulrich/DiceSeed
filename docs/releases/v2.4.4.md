# v2.4.4

The backup-verification quiz now checks every word (issue #14). Through
v2.4.3 it spot-checked 3 checkpoint words (~25%/60%/90% through the
phrase) — fast, but blind to any error in the 9 (12-word) or 21
(24-word) unchecked slots, and one wrong word in an unchecked slot is an
unrecoverable backup years later.

Per-word mechanics are unchanged: three blind candidates (the real word
plus two decoys), right/wrong feedback immediately after each pick,
progress shown as `n/12` or `n/24`. After the last word a new summary
screen reports "All N words verified. Your backup matches this seed." —
or lists every missed word number so you correct exactly those against
the word list — then returns to page 1.

Sensitive-data discipline is unchanged: the choices buffer stays three
slots, re-filled and scrubbed after every step; the widened state stores
word positions, never words. Full verification lengthens exposure time,
not surface. No change to the seed algorithm.
