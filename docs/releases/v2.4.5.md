# v2.4.5

The raw-entropy hex view becomes a flow stop on Touch boards (issue #15,
reshaped from the filed proposal: a screen in the path rather than a
third nav cell). After the last word page, `>` (or BTN2) now opens the
hex screen instead of the quiz; the hex screen carries the same `<` `>`
far-right nav stack, `<` returning to the words and `>` starting the
backup quiz — so seeing the entropy before verification is guaranteed,
not optional.

Issue #15's proposed third `#` cell below the `<` `>` stack was rejected:
three 48px cells do not fit the 170px screen height, and a linear flow
reads cleaner than a squeezed control.

Consequence: on Touch boards BTN1 no longer toggles words↔hex (flow-only
by design) — it mirrors `<` instead (back a page; back to words from the
hex screen; no-op on page 1 and on the quiz's right/wrong and summary
screens), keeping the buttons-mirror-cells rule. Non-touch boards are
unchanged: BTN1 keeps its words↔hex toggle, layout, and hints exactly as
since v2.0.0. No change to the seed algorithm.
